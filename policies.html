1. Shipping Policy

We proudly offer shipping both locally and nationwide within the United States, as well as across the Middle East. Because our products are entirely custom-made, please review our standard timelines:

2. Return & Refund Policy

At Pretty Moments, we pour our heart into creating bespoke favors tailored specifically for your event.

  • Custom-Made Items: Because all orders are customized with specific names, dates, colors, and fragrances, all sales are final once production begins. We cannot accept returns or exchanges for custom items.
