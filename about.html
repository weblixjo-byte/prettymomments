We believe event giveaways should be keepsakes of love, not just temporary items. Every candle, box, and ribbon is curated to spark a beautiful memory.

Crafted With Unconditional Love Since 2024

Pretty Moments was born out of a simple vision: to elevate event giveaways into works of art. In event planning, tiny details often have the biggest impact. We realized that standard event favors lacked personality, aroma, and premium presentation.

That is why we handcraft everything in-house. Using premium natural wax blends, clean aromatherapy-grade fragrances (like lavender, amber, musk, and vanilla), and real dried botanicals, our candles are finished with custom designed gold-foiled tag labels and velvet/silk ribbons.

From weddings to graduation scrolls and new baby celebrations, our favors act as luxury souvenirs for your guests, matching the high style and theme of your event.
